How we teach

On-site workshops

Hands-on practice with the portable spectrometer and the international iHerbSpec protocol, inside each herbarium. After the workshop, the project team stays on to measure specimens together with the local team.

3 to 5 days10 to 20 people

Online courses

The fundamentals of visible to near-infrared spectroscopy and its applications in botanical collections. Open to the whole network and to other institutions in the region.

Portuguese and Spanish

Open seminars

Regular talks on spectroscopy, taxonomy and biodiversity by experts from Brazil and abroad, streamed and recorded for anyone who wants to watch.

Every two monthsOnline

Data analysis

An international introductory course on spectral analysis and automated species identification, with programming practice, offered once the herbaria have measured their collections.

40 hOnline

Who the courses are for

Herbarium technicians, curators, undergraduate and graduate students, and researchers working with the Amazonian flora. No previous experience with spectroscopy is needed.

A shared protocol

Every workshop follows the same protocol for measuring herbarium specimens, based on the iHerbSpec Protocol, developed by an international community of herbaria. It covers everything from sample preparation and instrument calibration to recording spectra and their metadata. This is what allows data from different herbaria to be combined into a single collection.
